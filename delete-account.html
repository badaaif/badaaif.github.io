Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for apps published by Faisal Al-Badaai.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email

Send an email to contact@example.com with the subject “Account deletion request”, including the app name and the email address linked to your account. Requests are processed within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email).
  • Content you created in the app.
  • Associated usage data linked to your account.

What may be kept

  • Anonymous, aggregated crash and analytics data that cannot identify you.
  • Records required by law (for example, purchase records), kept for up to 90 days or as legally required.
