Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy applies to all Android apps published by Faisal Al-Badaai (“I”, “me”) on Google Play (the “Apps”). It explains what information the Apps collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Information collected

  • Information you provide. If an App offers an account or a contact form, it may collect your name, email address and any content you choose to submit.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Apps may collect anonymous crash reports and usage statistics (device model, OS version, app version, crash traces) to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Device permissions. Apps only request permissions needed for their features. Each permission is explained in the app before it is requested, and you can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

The Apps do not collect precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs or financial information unless clearly stated inside a specific App.

How information is used

  • To provide and maintain the App’s features.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • To respond to your support requests.

Your information is never sold.

Third-party services

Apps may use third-party services that process data under their own privacy policies, for example:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Crashlytics / Analytics)

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can request deletion of your data at any time; requests are processed within 30 days.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but reasonable measures are taken to protect your information.

Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, it will be deleted on request.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
